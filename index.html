Steal the Moment · Pathao vs Uber · Drop-off

Drop me at the gate, not on the highway.

Same destination, POL Depo Canteen Noya Kutum Bari in Dhaka Cantonment. Uber's route ends at the canteen. Pathao's pin lands on the Dhaka Elevated Expressway, so riders stop on New Airport Road (N3), across the highway. That happens on about half of my weekday office trips. Smart Drop-off fixes it with access-aware GPS snapping on the backend and gate coordination between passenger and rider.
