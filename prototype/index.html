    The problem

    Booking a bike to POL Depo Canteen Noya Kutum Bari (Dhaka Cantonment). Uber's route ends at the canteen. In Pathao, I can put the pin right on the canteen, but the route still ends on the highway (New Airport Road), across the road. The Dhaka Elevated Expressway runs right above the canteen's road, and bikes aren't allowed on it. This happens on about half of my office trips.

    Why not just fix the map or GPS?

    • The pin is already right. In "Today" the pin sits exactly on the canteen, and the ride still ends on the highway. Better GPS doesn't change that.
    • The map can't tell where riders can stop. Trip history can. Trips that ended well show where the real drop-off point is.
    • Today that knowledge lives in the passenger's head. You tell the rider "go left", he says the app shows straight on, and nothing is learned for the next trip.

    The fix: Smart Drop-off

    1. A drop-off point for each place. The route goes to where trips actually end well, not to the pin. It's learned from past trip endings: close to the place, with passengers tapping "right spot". New places start from the map's best guess and improve with every trip.
    2. Your landmark, said once. Instead of directing every rider at the turn, the passenger can add a landmark once, and the app remembers it.
    3. Rider gets both before accepting. The drop-off point and the landmark come with the ride request. If a passenger picks a spot on a highway, the rider is reminded to pull over safely.

    Why this is realistic

    • Ride apps already suggest pickup spots based on past trips. This applies the same idea to drop-off.
    • Pathao already records where every trip ends. That's the data the drop-off point is learned from.
    • Food delivery apps already send a saved note to the rider with each address.
    • At this canteen, the right road adds only a short turn for the rider (about 50 m).
