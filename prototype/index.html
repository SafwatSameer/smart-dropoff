    The problem

    Booking a bike to POL Depo Canteen Noya Kutum Bari (Dhaka Cantonment). Uber's route ends at the canteen. In Pathao, I can put the pin right on the canteen, but the route still ends on the highway (New Airport Road), across the road. The Dhaka Elevated Expressway runs right above the canteen's road, and bikes aren't allowed on it. This happens on about half of my office trips.

    Why not just fix the map or GPS?

    • The pin is already right. Try "Today": the pin sits exactly on the canteen, and the ride still ends on the highway. Better GPS doesn't change that.
    • Maps show roads, not the way in. Which side the gate is on, which small road to take: regulars know this, maps don't. A one-time tip passes it to every rider.
    • Riders end up calling. Today the rider phones to ask where to stop. With the spot and tip sent ahead, that call isn't needed.

    The fix: Smart Drop-off

    1. Better map logic. The ride never ends on a road bikes can't use. It ends on the nearest road they can use, close to your pin.
    2. Saved spot and tips. The passenger confirms the spot once and can add a tip in their own words. The app asks to remember both for next time.
    3. Better coordination. The rider gets the spot and the tip with the ride request, before accepting. If the spot is on a highway, the rider gets a short safety reminder.

    Why this is realistic

    • Food delivery apps already save a note for the rider with each address.
    • Map data already marks bridges and roads closed to motorcycles. OpenStreetMap tags this expressway that way.
    • The rider already gets the destination with the ride. We add the exact spot and the tip to it.
    • The right road adds only a short turn for the rider here (about 50 m).
