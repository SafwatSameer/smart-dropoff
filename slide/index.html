1The moment

I pin my office canteen. The rider stops across the highway.

✕ Pathao ends on the highway
✓ Uber ends at the canteen

About 1 in 2 of my weekday trips. Same place, same time.

2The bigger cause

The pin marks the place. Nothing marks where to stop.

Under flyoversAcross a dividerBack-road entrancesBig campuses

Same gap wherever the place and the stopping point differ. Better GPS won't fix it: the pin is already right.

3The fix: Smart Drop-off

Route to the drop-off point, not the pin.

  • Learn each place's drop-off pointFrom past trips that ended well.
  • Your landmark, said onceInstead of directing every rider.
  • Rider gets both before acceptingNo arguing at the turn.
